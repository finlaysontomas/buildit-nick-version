Notes for Tomas

Ideas from Dad

This page is for you, not for customers. It lists what this version adds to your BuildIT site, and why. The same notes are in the README. Take the bits you like.

What changed, and why

Tabs, one job per page

Home, Services, Work, Pricing, How it works, About, FAQ and Contact. Your live site is one long page, which is a good start. Separate pages let someone open Pricing or Work directly, and they show the shape of the business at a glance.

Your copy stays

BuildIT branding, the four-part offer, the four steps, Ember & Oat, Ironbark Electrical, Lux-Lashes (lux-lashes.com), Innovo (innovotech.com), the FAQ, the phone number and the email are yours. Prices are unchanged: Website $1,250 one-time, Website + Care $1,800 up front then $99 a month.

Pricing comparison table

The two cards are still there. The table lines up included and not included in words, because the Website + Care list is long and a table is easier to scan on a phone.

About you, and the Northern Beaches

Local customers want to know who they are emailing. The About page names you and positions BuildIT on Sydney's Northern Beaches. The bio box is a placeholder on purpose. I didn't invent a work history, client numbers or awards.

A contact form that doesn't pretend to send

The form is markup only. There is no backend, which is what you want on GitHub Pages. It opens an email draft. Phone and email links stay on the page for people who don't want a form.

A next step on every page

Each page ends with contact, call, or both. People decide on the page they are already reading. They shouldn't have to hunt for a button.

Mobile menu and contrast

Eight items don't fit in a phone header, so the menu becomes a button before the bar overflows, and the open menu scrolls. Your dark colours already clear WCAG AA for normal text (muted text, cyan, violet and magenta on the background are all above 4.5:1). The table still says "Included" and "Not included" in words.

Share preview image

Every public page has Open Graph and Twitter card tags pointing at your existing og-image.png, with width, height and alt text. Links in messages and social apps then show the BuildIT card instead of a blank preview.

Nothing made up

No testimonials, review scores, "trusted by" counts or awards. Where a sentence still needs your voice, it is marked Placeholder.
