How it works

Four simple steps

You tell us about your business. We design it, launch it on your domain, and keep the hosting running.

From first email to a live site

  1. 01

    You get in touch

    Tell us about your business, what you'd like customers to know, and any sites you like the look of. If you have a logo or photos, send them too. If not, that's fine.

  2. 02

    We design and build it

    We put together your site and send you a preview link. You tell us what to change, and we keep tweaking until you're happy with it.

  3. 03

    We launch it on your domain

    Once you give the go-ahead, your site goes live at your own web address, secured with HTTPS and ready to share with customers.

  4. 04

    We keep it running

    Your hosting is looked after. On Website + Care we also keep improving your site and handle changes for you, so it never goes stale.

What to have ready

Just the basics: what your business does, how customers can reach you, and your opening hours or service area if you have them. A logo and photos help, but if you don't have them, let us know and we'll talk through the options.

How long it takes depends on the size of your site and how quickly we can get your details and photos. Contact us about your business and we'll give you an idea of timing before we start.

After launch, your site stays online on the hosting we set up. On Website + Care we keep looking after it: monthly updates, backups, security updates and email support when you need a change. On the Website plan, your site is finished and live.
