Pricing

Two plans, clear prices

Pick the plan that suits you. You'll know exactly what you're paying before we start. Website is $1,250, paid once. Website + Care is $1,800 up front, then $99 a month for domain and hosting.

The two plans

Website

$1,250 one-time

One-time payment.

Just the website: we build your site, set up your domain and hosting, and launch it over HTTPS. Great if you want a solid site and don't need ongoing changes.

  • Custom website for your business
  • HTTPS secure (the padlock)
  • Your choice of .com or .com.au domain, set up and connected
  • Hosting, set up for you
  • Not included: Monthly updates
  • Not included: Domain and hosting renewals after year one

Everything included

$1,800 first payment

Then $99 a month for domain and hosting.

Everything a small business needs from its website, and we take care of all of it for you, before and after launch.
